Telecom Customer Service Policy - SIM Card & eSIM Replacement

Document ID: POL-SIM-2026-V3 | Effective Date: January 2026

1. Policy Overview & Scope

This policy governs customer support procedures for customers who have lost their mobile phone, suffered physical damage to their SIM card, or are experiencing 'No SIM / Invalid SIM' network errors.

2. Instant Digital eSIM Activation (Recommended First Option)

All customers with eSIM-capable smartphones (iPhone XR+, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 4+) are eligible for instant digital eSIM replacement free of charge ($0.00 fee). The customer service agent must verify the customer via registered email OTP or National ID number before generating the QR code. The eSIM profile is activated within 5 minutes over WiFi.

3. Physical SIM Card Replacement in Stores

If the customer does not have an eSIM-compatible device, they can pick up a triple-cut physical SIM card from any official telecom retail store. The standard replacement fee is $5.00. However, the $5.00 fee is automatically waived for VIP, Gold Tier customers, and customers who suffered SIM failure within 90 days of contract activation.

4. Urgent Line Suspension for Stolen Devices

When a customer reports their phone stolen, the agent must immediately apply an 'Urgent Outgoing Call & Data Bar' on the line to prevent unauthorized usage and bill shock, while keeping incoming SMS active for banking verification if requested.
