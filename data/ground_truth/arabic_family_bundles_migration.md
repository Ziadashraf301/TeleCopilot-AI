### Arabic Family Bundles Migration — Page 1

TELECOM EGYPT (WE) - CONSUMER COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS
POLICY DOCUMENT: WE MIX FAMILY BUNDLES, QUOTA SHARING & LINE OWNERSHIP TRANSFER
PAGE 1 OF 18 | CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC CUSTOMER SERVICE SPECIFICATION

1. WE MIX BUNDLE ARCHITECTURE & CORE BENEFITS (PAGE 1)
WE Mix combines Home Broadband (VDSL/FTTH) and mobile post-paid packages into a single consolidated billing statement, offering up to 25% savings compared to standalone subscriptions.

2. GIGABYTE QUOTA SHARING PROTOCOL
- The primary account owner (Group Master) allocates specific data caps (GBs) to secondary member lines via the My WE App or by calling 111.
- Intra-group voice minutes between all registered family lines are completely free of charge (0 EGP per minute).
- When any family member consumes 90% of their allocated quota, the Master line receives an automated SMS alert with one-click top-up options.

3. LINE OWNERSHIP TRANSFER PROCEDURES
- Both parties (Current Owner and New Assignee) must be physically present at an official WE Store.
- Both parties must present their valid, original Egyptian National Identification cards.
- All outstanding balances and administrative transfer fees (50 EGP inclusive of VAT) must be settled on the spot.
- Lines active for less than 90 days or lines with active handset installment contracts cannot be transferred until installments are fully paid.

4. PREPAID TO POSTPAID MIGRATION RULES
- Unused prepaid credit is automatically transferred as a credit balance on the first monthly postpaid bill.
- Customers migrating to Indigo plans receive double high-speed quota for the first 3 billing cycles.

TELECOM EGYPT (WE) - CONSUMER COMMERCIAL OPERATIONS
POLICY DOCUMENT: WE MIX FAMILY BUNDLES, QUOTA SHARING & LINE OWNERSHIP TRANSFER
PAGE 11 OF 18 | CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC CUSTOMER SERVICE SPECIFICATION

1. WE MIX BUNDLE ARCHITECTURE & CORE BENEFITS (PAGE 11)
WE Mix combines Home Broadband (VDSL/FTTH) and mobile post-paid packages into a single consolidated billing statement, offering up to 25% savings compared to standalone subscriptions.

2. GIGABYTE QUOTA SHARING PROTOCOL
- The primary account owner (Group Master) allocates specific data caps (GBs) to secondary member lines via the My WE App or by calling 111.
- Intra-group voice minutes between all registered family lines are completely free of charge (0 EGP per minute).
- When any family member consumes 90% of their allocated quota, the Master line receives an automated SMS alert with one-click top-up options.

3. LINE OWNERSHIP TRANSFER PROCEDURES
- Both parties (Current Owner and New Assignee) must be physically present at an official WE Store.
- Both parties must present their valid, original Egyptian National Identification cards.
- All outstanding balances and administrative transfer fees (50 EGP inclusive of VAT) must be settled on the spot.
- Lines active for less than 90 days or lines with active handset installment contracts cannot be transferred until installments are fully paid.

4. PREPAID TO POSTPAID MIGRATION RULES
- Unused prepaid credit is automatically transferred as a credit balance on the first monthly postpaid bill.
- Customers migrating to Indigo plans receive double high-speed quota for the first 3 billing cycles.
