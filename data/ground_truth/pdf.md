SECTION 1: DETAILED HARDWARE DIAGNOSTICS & TECHNICIAN DISPATCH SLA

1. CUSTOMER OUTAGE VERIFICATION PROTOCOL:
- Verify account status in CRM BSS. Confirm line is not administratively suspended for non-payment.
- Instruct customer to inspect the front LED lights of the VDSL / FTTH router.

2. OPTICAL FIBER FAULT (RED LOS LIGHT):
- If the LOS light is blinking RED, optical power has dropped below -28.0 dBm (Optical Loss of Signal).
- Advise customer NOT to bend or sharply pull the thin yellow/white optical cable.
- Create high-priority field repair ticket with SLA: 4 hours for Priority Accounts, 24 hours for Standard.

3. EMERGENCY 50GB MOBILE BACKUP DATA GUARANTEE:
- If the outage exceeds 2 hours during business working hours, immediately provision 50GB free 5G data
to the customer's registered WE mobile number valid for 72 hours.

4. TECHNICIAN DISPATCH & REPAIR REPORTING:
- Technicians must log arrival at premise, optical power meter reading, and root-cause component replacement.
- Zero fee policy for technical visits resulting from external network infrastructure faults.
