SECTION 1: HARDWARE DIAGNOSTICS FOR DN8245V, DG8045 & ZXHN H168N ROUTERS

1. VDSL LINE QUALITY VERIFICATION (192.168.1.1):
- Login using username 'admin' and printed serial password on router rear sticker.
- Check Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR Margin): Must remain above 8 dB for stability.
- Check Line Attenuation: Values below 20 dB indicate excellent copper distance.

2. RED INTERNET LED RESOLUTION:
- Verify PPPoE username ending in '@tedata.net.eg' and customer password.
- Trigger automated TR-069 WAN reconfiguration push from CSR Technical Support portal.

3. WI-FI FREQUENCY INTERFERENCE MANAGEMENT:
- Configure 2.4 GHz channels manually to 1, 6, or 11 to avoid adjacent channel bleed.
- Split 5 GHz and 2.4 GHz SSIDs to dedicate high bandwidth to streaming and gaming devices.

4. STORE HARDWARE REPLACEMENT CRITERIA:
- Faulty routers within active warranty are swapped instantly at any official WE Branch free of charge.
