### Cybersecurity Account Fraud Prevention — Page 1

TELECOM EGYPT (WE) - CYBERSECURITY & FRAUD DEFENSE DIRECTIVE
Document Ref: WE-SEC-FRAUD-2026-V1 | Page 1 of 18 | Classification: RESTRICTED

POLICY DOMAIN: SIM-SWAP FRAUD MITIGATION & OTP INTERCEPTION COUNTERMEASURES

1. EXECUTIVE FRAUD PROTOCOL (PAGE 1)
SIM-swap fraud occurs when malicious cybercriminals exploit social engineering or forged identity documents to seize control of a customer's cellular line, subsequently intercepting One-Time Passwords (OTPs) generated by banking institutions, digital wallets, and email services.

2. MANDATORY 24-HOUR FINANCIAL SMS LOCK (POLICY RULE SEC-88)
Whenever any SIM card is replaced (whether physical-to-physical or physical-to-eSIM):
- Outgoing and incoming standard cellular voice calls are activated immediately upon verification.
- ALL financial SMS notifications, banking OTPs, and high-risk transactional messages are subjected to a mandatory 24-hour quarantine hold.
- The customer receives an automated security SMS alert informing them of the replacement. If the replacement was unauthorized, the customer has a 2-hour window to freeze the line by replying with emergency code 'FREEZE'.

3. IN-PERSON BIOMETRIC & NATIONAL ID VERIFICATION
- Customer service representatives are strictly forbidden from executing SIM replacements remotely via phone or unverified online chat.
- The line owner must physically appear at an official WE retail branch with their valid, original Egyptian National ID card or diplomatic passport.
- Store agents must perform digital fingerprint scanning linked to the Civil Registry database prior to ICCID provisioning.

4. SOCIAL ENGINEERING & CALLER IMPERSONATION COUNTERMEASURES
If a caller contacts Customer Support claiming to be an IT Security Auditor or Law Enforcement asking for subscriber call logs:
- Immediately terminate the call and notify the Corporate Security & Internal Audit Department.
- Never disclose IMSI, PUK, or call detail records (CDRs) over voice channels.

---

**ANTI-FRAUD VERIFICATION FLOW - STAGE 1**

**Security Operational Standard**

| Phase 1: Incident Alert | Phase 2: Identity Verification | Phase 3: Emergency Lock |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Detect suspicious SIM swap or repeated failed logins within 15 minutes. | Perform multi-factor biometric or National ID authenticity audit. | Place immediate 24-hour security hold on line to protect bank 2FA OTPs. |

| Phase 4: Fraud Team Review | Phase 5: Account Cleared | Phase 6: Incident Logging |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Review recent CDRs, store CCTV, and digital authorization records. | In-person line owner verification required to restore banking SMS. | Submit compliance report to NTRA National Cyber Security Center (EG-CERT). |
