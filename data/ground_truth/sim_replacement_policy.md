Telecom Egypt (WE) - SIM Card Management & eSIM Migration Policy 
 
 
 
 
 Telecom Egypt (WE) - Customer Support Operational Policy 
 Document ID:  POL-SIM-2026-V4 |  Classification:  Customer Service Operations Manual 
 Effective Date:  January 1, 2026 |  Applies To:  All WE Customer Care Agents, Franchises & Corporate Stores 
 1. Executive Summary & Policy Scope 
 This operational directive provides definitive guidelines for Customer Service Representatives (CSRs) regarding physical SIM card replacements, digital eSIM provisioning, emergency lost or stolen line suspensions, and National ID biometric verification. Adherence to these protocols ensures client data privacy, anti-fraud compliance, and swift customer dispute resolution. 
 2. Immediate Stolen & Lost Line Suspension Protocol 
 When a customer contacts WE Customer Service reporting a stolen or missing mobile handset, the agent must execute immediate emergency barring without delay. 
 
 Step 1: Instant Outgoing Barring:  In CRM BSS, navigate to  Subscriber Services -> Emergency Actions  and select  Suspend Outgoing (OG) Calls & Data  immediately. This prevents fraudulent toll fraud and OTP interception. 
 Step 2: Caller Authentication:  The caller must verify at least 3 security checkpoints:
             
 Full 14-digit Egyptian National ID or Valid Passport Number registered on the account. 
 Last top-up or bill payment amount and transaction channel (Fawry, WE Pay, Credit Card). 
 Three frequently dialed numbers or primary package name. 
 
 
 Step 3: Full Two-Way Suspension:  Once verified, upgrade status to  Two-Way Full Barring (Incoming & Outgoing) . Line validity is preserved for 90 days during which no recurring package fees will be billed. 
 
 3. Physical SIM Card Replacement Rules & Fee Schedule 
 Customers can obtain a replacement Triple-Cut (Mini, Micro, Nano) SIM card at any official WE store or authorized reseller.

 4. Digital eSIM Provisioning & QR Migration Guidelines 
 eSIM technology allows customers to activate a cellular plan on supported devices without using a physical nano-SIM. CSRs must follow this exact transition procedure: 
 
 Eligibility Check:  Confirm the customer device EID (32-digit Embedded Identity Document) is whitelisted in WE BSS. Supported models include iPhone XS and later, Samsung Galaxy S20 and later, Google Pixel 4 and later.
     
 eSIM Migration Workflow: 
 
 Issuance Fee:  100 EGP for new eSIM issuance or physical-to-eSIM migration. 
 QR Code Delivery:  For security against SIM swap fraud, eSIM QR codes are issued via secure printed voucher at WE stores or sent via encrypted PDF to the customer's verified email after SMS 2FA verification. 
 Re-download Policy:  A QR code can be scanned up to 3 times on the same registered device IMEI/EID without additional fees. If transferred to a different physical phone, a new replacement fee (50 EGP) applies. 
 
 5. Third-Party Authorization & Legal Power of Attorney (POA) 
 In cases where the registered line owner cannot visit the store in person (e.g., travel, medical illness): 
 
 Strict Compliance Rule:  A first-degree relative (Father, Mother, Son, Daughter, Spouse) may request a replacement SIM only upon presenting an official General or Special Power of Attorney (توكيل رسمي موثق بالشهر العقاري) explicitly granting telecommunication line management rights, alongside original IDs of both parties.
     
 6. Mobile Number Portability (MNP) Inward Verification 
 For customers porting their numbers from Orange, Vodafone, or e& Egypt into WE: 
 
 The customer's national ID must match exactly with the donor operator records. 
 No outstanding overdue bills on postpaid accounts. 
 MNP process duration is strictly 24 working hours pursuant to NTRA regulations. 
 
 7. Troubleshooting Inactive Replacement SIMs 
 If the customer reports "No Service" or "SIM Not Provisioned MM#2" following replacement: 
 
 Check HLR/HSS status in network diagnostics tool. Verify IMSI mapping matches the ICCID printed on the card. 
 Instruct customer to toggle Airplane Mode for 30 seconds or perform hard reboot. 
 If unprovisioned after 1 hour, trigger automated HLR re-synchronization in CSR Portal.
