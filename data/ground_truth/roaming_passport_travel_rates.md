## Operational rules

Rule: Postpaid consumer accounts require a minimum active tenure of 3 months or a 1,000 EGP refundable deposit to activate international roaming services. VIP Indigo Gold and Platinum tiers enjoy instant zero-deposit activation.
  [id]: R-ROAM-01
  [name]: Automatic Deposit & Credit Limit Requirement
Rule: Pursuant to NTRA consumer protection mandates, data roaming without an active Roaming Passport bundle is hard-capped at 250 EGP per billing day unless the customer explicitly opts in via SMS to continue at pay-as-you-go rates.
  [id]: R-ROAM-02
  [name]: Data Roaming Bill Shock Protection
Rule: Customers can subscribe before departure or upon arrival by dialing *202# or via My WE App. Roaming Passport bundles activate immediately upon first data usage or outgoing call in the covered destination.
  [id]: R-ROAM-03
  [name]: Roaming Passport Bundle Activation
